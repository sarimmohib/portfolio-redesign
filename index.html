01 / About

A student who ships.

Most of my time goes into understanding how things actually work — then building small, useful tools with what I learn. The goal is never more features; it's fewer, better ones.

Right now that means Android — calm, considered apps made with Kotlin — and agent tooling, where a handful of small, well-defined pieces can do the work of one big one. I like clean interfaces, honest software, and details you only notice after a week of use.
